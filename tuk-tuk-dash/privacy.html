Summary

Tuk-Tuk Dash is an offline single-player game. The MVP does not create accounts, does not require a network connection to play, and does not include ads or in-app purchases.

Data we store on your device

DataWherePurpose
Best scoreLocal storage on device (localStorage / WebView)Show high score between sessions
Language preferenceLocal storage on deviceRemember EN / Khmer UI choice
Mute stateLocal storage on deviceRemember mute preference

We do not upload scores, contacts, location, device identifiers, personal/financial/health information, messages, photos, audio, files, or browsing/activity data in the MVP build.

Optional font resource

When a network is available, the HTML may request Google Fonts for Khmer/Latin rendering. Gameplay remains usable offline through local system-font fallbacks; the app does not upload scores, preferences, contacts, location, or identifiers.

What we do not do (MVP)

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics / crash SDKs in the MVP web build
  • No advertising ID collection in MVP
  • No selling personal data
  • No tracking across apps or websites

Children

The game is a casual endless runner with no chat, UGC, or gambling. Suitable for a general audience. Parents: gameplay is offline; no social features.

Future changes

If we later add optional ads, analytics, or cloud features, this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the Play listing will be revised accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact grangerover@gmail.com.
