1. What this app is

Local Gigs PP is a listing board for short informal gigs in Phnom Penh. People post needs or offer skills, then contact each other outside the app (Telegram, WhatsApp, phone). In the current build, posts are stored only on your own device.

2. We are not an employer or agency

The publisher is not an employer, recruiter, staffing agency, broker, or party to any gig, agreement, or payment between users. We do not set rates, supervise work, insure anyone, or guarantee that a gig, worker, or payment is real.

3. No user vetting

We do not vet, verify, screen, or background-check users or posts. Listings marked SAMPLE are fake demo data. You are responsible for judging who you deal with.

4. Off-app messaging and payment are at your own risk

All messaging, meetings, and payments happen outside the app and are your own responsibility and risk. There is no escrow, wallet, or deposit protection. Meet in public first, share your exact address only once you trust the chat, and never send deposits or “holding fees” to strangers.

5. Prohibited uses

  • Sex work, sexual services, escorting, “massage with extras,” or any adult/sexual content
  • Anything involving minors — the app is 18+ only; do not post or accept gigs involving children
  • Illegal goods or services (e.g. drugs, weapons, fake documents, organ sales, scams, money mules, trafficking)
  • Harassment, discrimination, impersonation, or posting other people’s contact details without consent

The app blocks some obvious prohibited words when you create a post. That check runs only on your phone; it cannot see chats, other devices, or content shared elsewhere, so it is not full moderation. Report abuse to grangerover@gmail.com and, where a crime is involved, to local authorities.

6. No warranty, limited liability

The app is provided “as is,” without warranties of any kind. To the extent allowed by law, the publisher is not liable for losses, injuries, disputes, or damages arising from gigs, meetings, messages, or payments between users.

7. Changes

We may update these terms. When they change materially, the app asks you to accept again. See also the Privacy policy.
