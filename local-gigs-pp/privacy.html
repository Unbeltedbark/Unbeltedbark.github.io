Who we are

Local Gigs PP is published under the display name .//Talentless/x/Hack. Product contact for privacy questions: grangerover@gmail.com.

What the app does

A thin board to browse and post short informal gigs, then contact people on Telegram, WhatsApp, or phone outside the app. There is no escrow, no in-app wallet, and no live dispatch.

Data stored on your device

In the current build, data stays in your browser / WebView localStorage on the device:

  • gigAgg.localPosts — gig posts and skill offers you create
  • gigAgg.lang — language preference (English / Khmer)
  • gigAgg.hideDemo / gigAgg.reviewMode — Hide SAMPLE (screenshot / review mode)
  • gigAgg.installTipDismissed — old Add to Home Screen tip dismissed (legacy, before v0.13)
  • gigAgg.firstOpenDismissed — first-open welcome note dismissed (v0.13+)
  • gigAgg.lastKhan — the khan you picked on your last post, so the post form can pre-fill it (local only)
  • gigAgg.savedStars — ids of gigs you starred / saved on this device
  • gigAgg.hiddenGigs — ids you hid via “Not interested / Hide on this device” (local only; distinct from stars)
  • gigAgg.deviceId — random id so “My posts” works on the same browser
  • gigAgg.termsAccepted — Terms & Disclaimer version you accepted and when (local only)

Demo SAMPLE listings ship as a static JSON file inside the app package (fake contacts, labeled SAMPLE). Offline-banner dismiss is session-only (not written to localStorage). “Export my posts” downloads a JSON file you choose to save; it does not upload data. Clearing site data removes local posts, stars, hidden ids, and preferences.

When you publish, the app checks your text against a short prohibited-content word list on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere; blocked text is simply not saved. The “Sponsored · reserved space” boxes are empty placeholders — no ad network, ad script, or tracking is loaded.

What we do not do (this build)

  • No cloud user accounts or passwords
  • No sale of personal data
  • No analytics SDKs baked into this static build
  • No payment card, Bakong, or KHQR settlement inside the app
  • No server-side chat or message storage

Third parties

Contact actions open Telegram, WhatsApp, or the phone dialer at your request — those apps have their own policies. KHQR images appear only on legacy SAMPLE / pre-v0.13 posts (display only) and may load from a third-party host. The post form no longer collects a KHQR URL; there are no in-app payments — people settle outside the app. Hosting this file on GitHub Pages (or similar) means the host’s standard web logs may apply to page requests.

User content & off-app messaging

Titles, descriptions, and contact handles you post are user-generated content stored on your device in this build. SAMPLE seed rows ship in the app package and are labeled. Messaging happens only after you confirm leaving the app — we do not store or moderate Telegram/WhatsApp chats. If you suspect a scam: leave the chat, block the sender, and do not send money.

Children

Intended for adults 18+. Do not post or accept gigs involving minors.

Shared board (when configured)

When Reed fills data/feed-config.json, posting can also send your gig text and contact link to a Google Form, stored in a Google Sheet. Those posts (including contact links) are public to anyone who opens the app with the shared board on. Google’s policies apply to Form/Sheet data. With an empty config, behaviour stays on-device only (Phase A). Extra keys: gigAgg.sharedFeedCache, gigAgg.feedConfigCache.

This is a soft-launch Phnom Penh board — not a live city-wide marketplace claim.

Request removal

Delete your local copy in My posts. To remove a shared Sheet row, email grangerover@gmail.com with the post id (or use in-app Report when configured). Reed can set status to hidden/removed or delete the row.

Future backends

Further backends beyond the optional Form/Sheet above will be documented here the same day they ship. Empty feed-config = on-device storage + static assets only.

Contact / deletion

Delete your posts in-app (My posts), or clear the site’s browser data. Questions: grangerover@gmail.com.
